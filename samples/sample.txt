Kickoff notes, taken on a phone in a hallway.

We agreed the import flow should feel boring. You pick a file, you wait less
than a second, and you land in the editor with your cursor already in the
document. No preview step, no confirmation dialog, no "choose how to map your
styles" wizard.

Plain text files get the simplest possible treatment. Blank lines separate
paragraphs. A single newline inside a paragraph stays a line break, because
people wrap addresses and poetry by hand and expect that to hold.

Anything that looks like markup in a .txt file is text, not markup. If someone
pastes <b>this</b> into their notes, they should see the angle brackets.
